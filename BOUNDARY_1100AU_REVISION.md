# Unified 1100 AU transport boundary

The configuration key `outer_boundary_radius_au` defaults to `1100.0`. It is the **one** configurable outer surface for incident sampling, unbound outgoing recording, and bound-orbit removal. The old `outer_removal_radius_rsun` key is rejected with a migration message. The numerical/analytic matching surface is the solar surface, `1 R_sun`; there is no separate injection-radius constant.

At the outer sphere the focused, solar-intersecting incident ensemble retains the asymptotic SHM speed law proportional to `f(u) [u + v_esc²(R_sun)/u]`. The local speed is `sqrt(u² + v_esc²(R_outer))`, and the transverse impact parameter is sampled uniformly in area up to `R_sun sqrt[u² + v_esc²(R_sun)] / v_outer`. The initial position is exactly on the inward crossing of the outer sphere. A hyperbolic Kepler map transports it to the solar surface before numerical collision propagation.

Exact hyperbolic shell moments for the incoming path, `dt` and `v²dt`, are stored in `incident_inbound.tsv` for every successfully propagated incident particle. The transport `radial_blocks.tsv` also stores these moments by statistical block. An unscattered complete transit combines this incoming path, the simulated solar crossing, and the outgoing Kepler path back to the same outer sphere. The incoming and post-escape paths are separate from **captured** residence, which begins at first capture and ends at validated escape or outer removal. Adding pre-capture time to captured residence would invalidate the capture-rate normalization.

The native radial grid is unchanged through `1.1 R_sun` at `0.001 R_sun` spacing. Beyond that, widths grow by 2% and are capped at `1 AU`, making the enlarged radial domain practical; its last shell is clipped at the configured outer radius. The upper `0.1 R_sun` of the native grid is now populated by analytic exterior arcs where applicable. Changing the outer boundary changes both the sampling reference and the orbit-removal prescription, so boundary-sensitivity runs must compare independent capture and transport samples using the same setting in each pair.

The transport product schema is version 7. `metadata.json` contains `R_match_rsun=1` and `R_outer_au`; the earlier `R_inj_rsun` and `R_remove_rsun` fields are absent. `radial_blocks.tsv` adds `incident_inbound_dt_s` and `incident_inbound_v2dt_km2_s` after the previous ten columns. `incident_inbound.tsv` is also present for fixed-incident Capture runs. Version-6 products remain historical and are rejected by the version-7 analysis scripts; they must not be relabeled as new-boundary results.

This rule has no planetary perturbations and no finite-age cutoff. Since `1100 AU` is much farther than the former `1100 R_sun` removal surface, new production results require fresh convergence and planetary-sensitivity checks. The archived schema-6 validation results in `TRANSPORT_REVISION_20260918.md` establish the earlier implementation only.
