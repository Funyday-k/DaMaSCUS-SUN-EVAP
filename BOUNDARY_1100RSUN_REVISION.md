# Separate incident, matching, diagnostic, and removal radii

Schema 8 restores the Sun-only bound-orbit cutoff to `outer_removal_radius_rsun = 1100.0` (about 5.1 AU). A bound orbit whose aphelion reaches that cutoff contributes only the outward arc up to the first crossing, then receives the `outer_orbit_removed` outcome. This is a model-validity prescription for orbits sensitive to planetary perturbations; it does not simulate those perturbations or count removal as evaporation. Cutoff runs use 550, 1100, and 2200 **solar radii**. The native grid extends from 0 to 1.1 solar radii in 0.001-solar-radius shells and then grows by 2% up to a 10-solar-radius width cap.

The focused solar-intersecting incident ensemble is drawn from asymptotic halo speeds and represented at a fixed 1100 AU sphere. Its Kepler state is mapped through 2 solar radii to the 1-solar-radius numerical matching surface. The intermediate 2-solar-radius state is a deterministic injection checkpoint; scattering propagation begins at the solar surface. Thus changing the bound removal cutoff does not change incident sampling or the solar collision initial state. The legacy `outer_boundary_radius_au` configuration key is rejected to prevent a silent unit error.

`incident_inbound.tsv` and the last two columns of `radial_blocks.tsv` retain exact incoming `dt` and `v²dt` moments, separately from post-capture residence. Their diagnostic outer reference is `min(1100 R_sun, R_remove)`, so the reference remains within the native recorded grid even in the 550-solar-radius cutoff run. Unscattered transit and post-escape outgoing columns terminate at the same diagnostic reference. These columns describe the selected solar-intersecting sample, not the full Galactic halo density. The analytic Kepler map from 1100 AU to the diagnostic reference is used to set initial states but is **not** included in the native-grid transit histogram. Metadata names both the sampling sphere and the diagnostic reference so this scope is explicit.

Schema-8 `metadata.json` records `R_inj_rsun`, `R_match_rsun`, `R_incident_au`, `R_transit_reference_rsun`, and `R_remove_rsun` separately, plus interpolation grid size, RK position/velocity/phase tolerances, and optical-depth controls. The point analyzer requires matching capture/transport numerical settings, matching radial grids, independent RNG streams, and exact block closure. `derived.json` now reports `jackknife_bias` and `jackknife_bias_corrected` alongside the existing point estimate and standard error. For every nonlinear observable, the bias diagnostic adds the independent capture and transport delete-block estimates `(B-1)(mean(X_delete)-X_full)`. It is a finite-sample diagnostic, not a substitute for sample-size or radial-bin convergence.

Schema-6 and schema-7 products are historical and rejected by schema-8 analysis. Current validation runs are kept under `validation-results/boundary_1100rsun_20260918/` and `validation-results/boundary_1100rsun_500_20260918/` (ignored by Git); their exact status and limitations are recorded there. No result from the prior 1100 AU removal model is relabeled as a result of this model.
